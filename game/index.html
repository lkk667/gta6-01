AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME GAME

Mara Vega owes the Ferraro crew $4,000, and Solmar City always collects. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD, and work five jobs from the beach to the harbor to buy your freedom.

Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car. Traffic, pedestrians, police chases, missions, combat — and a wanted level that follows you everywhere.
